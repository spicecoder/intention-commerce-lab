BEGIN WITH THE SITUATION

Before code,
a situation.

A person has arrived. A customer identifier is available. Something is being sought.

Most programs begin with a function to call or an event to handle. Intention Space begins one step earlier: what is the situation asking participants to notice, and for what purpose?

Observation is not yet action. Seeing a customer identifier does not say whether to view a profile, assess stock, close an account, or create an order.

PURPOSE COMPLETES THE COMMUNICATION

A payload is not the whole message.

A conventional message often leaves purpose implicit in an endpoint, topic, prompt, handler, or call stack. An Intention Space Signal keeps purpose and observation together as a complete semantic unit.

COOPERATION WITHOUT ASSUMED RELIABILITY

A returned tool call is not proof of purpose fulfilled.

Traditional agents inherit the reliability assumptions of their tools. Intention Space makes those assumptions visible at every transition.

The guard does not eliminate bugs or hallucinations. It constrains their silent propagation into action.
