DISTRIBUTED ATTENDANCE / AN ILLUSTRATIVE MAPPING

A resource-aware attention cycle.

Each domain offers a resource cost for attendance. An attendance DN computes the lowest-cost cycle through every required domain; local DNs then receive attention under their own governing Space. This optimizer does not replace causal business ordering.

Balanced uses neutral weight 1. Pressure profiles add a declared surcharge to legs connected to each weighted domain. Weights change route order and connected-leg costs; they do not skip domains or represent reliability.
